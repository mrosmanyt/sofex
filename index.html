How we make it

Three things we refuse to cut corners on

01 — RAW MATERIAL

Fresh surfactant, not recovered stock

Sulphonic acid, SLES and thickener come from documented suppliers against batch certificates. We do not buy reclaimed or blended-down chemical — the single biggest reason cheap local dishwash goes cloudy and loses foam on the shelf.

02 — BATCH CONTROL

Every batch tested before filling

Each mix is checked for pH, active matter, viscosity and clarity before a single bottle is filled, and the batch number is printed on the pack. A failed batch is reworked — it does not quietly go out at a discount.

03 — HONEST FILL

450ml means 450ml

Volumetric fill heads are calibrated at the start of every shift and audited mid-run. Short-filling is common in this category and it is the fastest way to lose a retailer's trust, so we measure it and keep the records.
